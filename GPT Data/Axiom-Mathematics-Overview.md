**Overview:**  
Incremental learning (IL) is crucial for AGI because it allows models to continually update and adapt to new tasks and changing environments without forgetting previous knowledge. However, current IL approaches in LLMs face significant challenges:

- **Batch-Incremental vs. Real-Time Learning:** Most methods update models in batches rather than in a seamless, real-time manner.
- **Catastrophic Forgetting:** Without robust techniques, new training can degrade or erase previously learned knowledge.
- **Scalability and Efficiency:** Many methods (e.g., regularization, replay, adapters) either introduce high computational costs or fail to generalize across diverse tasks.
- **Limited Adaptability:** Many IL methods adjust only peripheral parts of the model (like prompts or adapters) rather than updating the core knowledge representations.

**Key Findings:**

- Current methods often treat IL as a periodic update rather than a continuous, autonomous process.
- For true lifelong learning, models must be capable of dynamic, real-time updates that preserve and integrate past knowledge seamlessly.
