## 2) What the text actually is: conlangs, anti‑languages, and transliteration engines

Two concurrent readings exist, and they’re not mutually exclusive:

**(A) Conlang/cryptolect design.**
FL uses **anti‑languages** (in‑group codes) and cryptolects as a core concept—NeoGAF’s thread framed the output as “anti‑languages” designed to communicate in‑group and **exclude outsiders**. The label set itself includes “Cryptolect.” [![](https://t0.gstatic.com/faviconV2?client=SOCIAL&type=FAVICON&fallback_opts=TYPE,SIZE,URL&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.neogaf.com&size=32&drop_404_icon=true)NeoGAF](https://www.neogaf.com/threads/forgotten-languages-website.1654526/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)[![](https://t0.gstatic.com/faviconV2?client=SOCIAL&type=FAVICON&fallback_opts=TYPE,SIZE,URL&url=https%3A%2F%2Fforgottenlanguages-full.forgottenlanguages.org&size=32&drop_404_icon=true)forgottenlanguages-full.forgottenlanguages.org](https://forgottenlanguages-full.forgottenlanguages.org/p/books-2021.html)

**(B) Transliteration/cipher overlays.**
Independent researchers documented that many FL passages **map 1‑to‑1 onto English sources** via **systematic letter/word substitution**; “stones” (unchanged citations, biblical verse references, etc.) are used as anchors to reconstruct the mapping. The best public breakdown lives on **Strange Minds**, which shows a step‑by‑step “stone‑hunting” method and even mentions **Microsoft Transliteration Utility** and language‑specific two‑column mapping tables. [![](https://t0.gstatic.com/faviconV2?client=SOCIAL&type=FAVICON&fallback_opts=TYPE,SIZE,URL&url=https%3A%2F%2Fstrangeminds.au&size=32&drop_404_icon=true)strangeminds.au](https://strangeminds.au/forgottenlanguages-the-deepest-rabbithole-on-the-internet/)

**FL’s own description (early):** On **Lingvoforum (2011)** the handle **Ayndryl** describes **Nodespaces** and **Vectorial**—language drift modeled with **Ising‑style dynamics**, critical exponents (β), and “Weddag‑2075” as a future evolved Welsh. Whether this is literal tooling or performative math‑fiction, it’s a coherent, publishable story about **simulation‑driven language evolution**. [![](https://t0.gstatic.com/faviconV2?client=SOCIAL&type=FAVICON&fallback_opts=TYPE,SIZE,URL&url=https%3A%2F%2Flingvoforum.net&size=32&drop_404_icon=true)Лингвофорум](https://lingvoforum.net/index.php?topic=30262.50)

**Bottom line:** The “languages” range from **rich, internally consistent cryptolects** to **ciphers/transliterations** of English texts. FL blurs art, code, and linguistics on purpose.
