## **Core Tenets and Teachings of Shiva**

Lord Shiva teaches a path of balance between extremes—renunciation and enjoyment, detachment and compassion. His central tenets include:

- **Non-Dualism (Advaita)**: Shiva represents the ultimate reality—pure consciousness (Shiva-consciousness), transcending all dualities and distinctions. 🌌
- **Yoga & Meditation**: He's seen as the first yogi, teaching that liberation (Moksha) is achieved through meditation, self-discipline, and control over one's desires and senses. 🕉️🧘‍♂️
- **Detachment & Renunciation**: Shiva encourages detachment from material existence, ego, and illusion (Maya), thus attaining spiritual enlightenment. 🌬️🍃
- **Acceptance of Impermanence**: Shiva embodies the inevitability of change, reminding devotees to accept life’s cyclical nature—creation, preservation, and dissolution—as eternal cosmic rhythms. 🌀⏳
- **Equality & Compassion**: Shiva, as Mahadev ("Great God"), sees all beings equally, often depicted embracing societal outcasts, animals, and spirits, symbolizing universal compassion. 💞🌍
