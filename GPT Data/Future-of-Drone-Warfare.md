## 6. Recommendations



- **Invest in counters** – Directed‑energy, wide‑area EW and layered hard‑kill/soft‑kill defences should be funded as aggressively as the drones themselves.

- **Hybrid force design** – Pair every manned platform with cheap, sacrificial robotic wingmen, boats or ground vehicles; think “Swiss‑army knife swarm.”

- **Codify autonomy rules** – Push for an UN‑like convention on autonomous lethal decision‑making to pre‑empt a virtual nuclear‑arms race in AI.

- **Battle‑lab learning loops** – Ukraine and Gaza prove data is the new uranium; capture, share and refine tactics before the next adversary does.
